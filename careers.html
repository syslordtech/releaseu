Apply as a listener

What you'll do

  1. Listen with respect and without judgement.
  2. Stay calm if someone is distressed, and point them to 112 or Tele-MANAS 14416.
  3. Use the report and block tools whenever a call feels unsafe.

What you won't do

  1. Claim to be a therapist, or give diagnosis or medical advice.
  2. Promise secrecy if someone may be in danger.
  3. Ask for personal contact details or money outside the app.

How applying works

  1. Create a listener account and fill in your languages and a short introduction.
  2. Upload a government ID. It is stored privately and only approved reviewers can open it.
  3. Our team reviews your application. You cannot take calls until you are approved.
  4. Once approved, go online and start listening.

Listener terms, tax details and your working relationship with Release-U will be shared in full before you accept.
